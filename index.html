What the model measures

Spatial distance. Euclidean distance between two occupied points: √((x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²), in metres. People sharing a point have zero spatial separation.

Social distance. The fewest friendship edges between two people. Same person = 0; direct friends = 1; disconnected = ∞. This is a metric within a connected group and an extended metric across disconnected groups. Locations inherit their occupants’ social relationships; empty locations have no social distance.

Walking distance. A shortest route on a 2 m grid, simplified with collision-checked shortcuts around the pond and buildings. Approximate, not the exact continuous shortest path. Animation follows these routes at 1.4 m/s, accelerated by the selected speed.

Reading “near” and “far.” This experiment uses ≤40 m for spatially near and >40 m for far; ≤1 link for socially close, 2 for intermediate and ≥3 for distant. These are adjustable ideas to discuss, not universal human thresholds. Every person, friendship and place here is fictional.
